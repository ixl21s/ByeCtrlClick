License management improvements — no activation limit, no keychain prompt, and clearer license details.

## Improvements

- **No more activation limit** – Your license now works without the 3-device cap. Reinstalls or switching Macs will no longer lock you out.
- **No more keychain prompt** – The license is now stored differently, so the annoying "enter password" dialog on launch is gone.
- **License details** – "Show Key" now also reveals the full purchase email tied to your license.
- Minor wording improvements in license management
