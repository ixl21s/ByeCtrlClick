1. [Download the latest `.dmg`](https://updates.byectrlclick.com/downloads/ByeCtrlClick-1.6.1.dmg) and drag **ByeCtrlClick** into your **Applications** folder.  
2. Launch ByeCtrlClick and follow the onboarding.

4. Enter your licence key from Gumroad – done.
